## Cycle and capacity

> **▸ Fill:** the planning cycle length (e.g. six weeks, a quarter), the capacity unit teams plan in (engineer-weeks, points, team-cycles), and how much capacity is normally held back for maintenance and unplanned work.

## Sizing scale

<!-- crew:discoverable capability=mcp:tracker -->

> **▸ Fill:** the relative sizing scale and what each step means in this org (T-shirt sizes, Fibonacci points, days). If the tracker has an estimate field, its allowed values are the starting point.

## Item hierarchy

<!-- crew:discoverable capability=mcp:tracker -->

> **▸ Fill:** the names for each level, top to bottom — e.g. theme → initiative → epic → story → task — and which levels live in the tracker. If the tracker is connected, its issue types and parent links show which levels exist.

## Where drafts go

> **▸ Fill:** where planning drafts are written (a directory in a repo, a docs space) and the file-naming pattern. Generic default: `planning/drafts/<kind>-<slug>.md` in the working repository.

## Narrative format

> **▸ Fill:** the narrative shape your org prefers for idea and theme write-ups — its section headings in order, or a link to the template — and any length limit. idea-shaping falls back to problem → proposal → open questions while this is empty.

## Vocabulary

> **▸ Fill:** local words a planning agent must not get wrong — what your org calls a cycle, a bet, an appetite, a milestone — and any word that means something different here than elsewhere.
